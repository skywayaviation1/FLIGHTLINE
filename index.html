FAA Part 135 operator

Part 135 certificated.
Deliberately focused.

Skyway operates under an FAA Part 135 air carrier certificate, and we concentrate on three proven types. A narrow fleet means deeper parts support, crews current on the aircraft they fly, and fewer surprises on the day of the trip.

Approved operations specifications

FAA-issued ops specs define what we fly, where we fly it, and the conditions we can operate in. Nothing happens outside them.

Trained and tested crews

Recurrent training and checkrides, Part 135 flight, duty, and rest limits, and a DOT drug and alcohol testing program.

Continuous maintenance program

Aircraft are maintained on an approved inspection program, tracked continuously and signed off before the trip releases.
